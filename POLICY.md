# Release License Policy

A release bundle may be cleared for public distribution only when every upstream asset
in the bundle carries a license from the allow-list below.

## Allow-list

- Apache-2.0
- MIT
- BSD-2-Clause
- BSD-3-Clause
- ODC-BY (Open Data Commons Attribution License)
- CC-BY-4.0

## Everything else blocks the bundle

Any non-commercial (NC) license, any share-alike (SA) license, any copyleft license
(GPL/AGPL), and any asset whose license is `unknown`, `other`, missing or unlicensed
must keep the bundle on hold.
